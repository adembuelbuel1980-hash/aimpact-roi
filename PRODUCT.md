# Aimpact — Product context

## Purpose
Aimpact is a Zurich-based, founder-led service for local restaurants and cafés. The website's primary job is to generate qualified requests for a free personal Google Maps check, then convert appropriate prospects into a paid 90-day implementation project.

## Audience
Owners and operators of restaurants and cafés in Zurich, typically time-constrained and not digital-marketing specialists. Other local businesses are secondary.

## Primary conversion
"Gratis Maps-Check anfragen" -> anchor #maps-anfrage -> Formspree contact form. Keep this route working and make it unmistakable. A separate self-service technical website check at https://check.aimpact.ch/website-check is secondary.

## Offer
A free personal Maps check reviews the Google Business Profile, three local search terms and three competitors, with three improvement priorities and a stated 24-hour response. Paid 90-day implementation currently listed at CHF 2’490 or three instalments of CHF 830. Do not change price, scope, promised response time, warranties or legal terms without approval.

## Positioning and voice
Personal, precise, locally relevant, understandable Swiss Standard German (de-CH), no inflated promises or jargon. Emphasise Google Maps visibility, accurate business information, fast paths to reservations and measurable enquiries. Never guarantee a Google position, AI citation or a specific number of new guests.

## Non-negotiables
- Keep existing forms, Formspree endpoint, GA4 tag, assistant script, consent copy, privacy and imprint links functional.
- Preserve primary CTA destination #maps-anfrage and technical check URL.
- Retain brand's charcoal/black, restrained gold and warm white style.
- Prioritise mobile usability, clear information hierarchy, accessible keyboard interaction, speed and legibility.
- Avoid unnecessary redesign, new dependencies or heavy animations.
- Never merge into main or publish without review.
